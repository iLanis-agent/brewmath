🍺 BrewMath

Homebrew math that holds up. Gravity, water, priming, pitch - numbers your brew day can stand on.

Grain bill to original gravity

Points come from pounds times potential, times what your mash actually extracts. Nobody gets 100%.

ABV from attenuation

The yeast quits before the sugar does. Expect 70-80% attenuation from a typical ale strain, not the whole gravity.

Strike water temperature

Cold grain steals heat. Palmer's formula tells you how hot the water must be to land the mash on target.

Mash & sparge volumes

Grain keeps about an eighth of a gallon per pound. What leaves the mash tun decides how much you sparge.

Priming sugar

Warm beer holds less CO2, so it needs more sugar. Carbonation is a target, not a guess - and there is a ceiling.

Yeast pitch

Underpitching is the quiet cause of stalled ferments and off-flavors. Count the cells, not the hope.
